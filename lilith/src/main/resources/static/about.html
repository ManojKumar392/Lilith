Lilith is a community-driven mapping platform that transforms location-based safety reports into visual geographic insights.

How Lilith Works

1

Select a Location

Choose a location directly on the interactive map.

2

Submit a Report

Select the reason and severity and optionally describe the issue.

3

Reports Are Grouped

Reports are automatically assigned to fixed geographic grid cells based on their coordinates.

4

Patterns Become Visible

Reports in each grid cell are aggregated to produce a severity score displayed on the map.

Severity Model

Every report contributes points according to the severity selected by the reporter.

Low 1 point
Medium 2 points
High 3 points

The accumulated score determines whether a reported grid cell appears yellow, orange, or red. Areas with no reports are left unclassified rather than being assumed to be safe.

Data Design

Lilith stores individual reports as the underlying data. Each report is associated with a user and a geographic grid cell.

Severity scores and map classifications are derived from the reports rather than stored as permanent values. This allows the visualization to remain consistent as new reports are added.
